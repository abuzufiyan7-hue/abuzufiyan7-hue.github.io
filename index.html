About

What PRView.site does

PRView.site is a simple, independent shortcut from a username to a profile — nothing more, nothing less.

Type an Instagram username into the field above and PRView.site checks that the format is valid, then opens that account's profile page on instagram.com in a new tab. The whole interaction takes a few seconds and requires no account here.

  1. 01

    Enter the username

    Type it exactly as it appears on Instagram — with or without the leading @. Periods and underscores are fine.

  2. 02

    PRView.site validates the format

    Usernames may contain letters, numbers, periods and underscores, up to 30 characters. Invalid input is flagged before anything opens.

  3. 03

    Instagram opens in a new tab

    You land on the profile URL at instagram.com. Everything you see there — including whether the account is public or private — is controlled by Instagram.

PRView.site never asks for an Instagram password, and there is nowhere on this site to enter one. There is no login, no session, and no stored search history.

Profile visibility is decided by Instagram and by the account owner. Private accounts remain private, and content that is restricted on Instagram stays restricted here as well. PRView.site only points your browser at a public URL — it cannot reveal anything Instagram does not already show.
